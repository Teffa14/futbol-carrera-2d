# Argentina football overhaul

This slice changes rules, spatial behavior, goalkeeper career support, build identity and match UX together.

- Law 11 snapshots every attacker who is offside when the pass is played and only resets on a new attacking phase or deliberate defensive play.
- The striker uses one legal offside edge, with connect, pin, check, depth and box-arrival modes instead of stacked safety margins.
- Builds have runtime effects on decisions, acceleration, fatigue, skill moves, passing, finishing and duels.
- Goalkeeper is a full career position with three dedicated styles and stat-derived overall.
- Goalkeepers predict shot lines, sweep through balls, cushion manageable shots and parry difficult ones.
- Horizontal carrying uses physical side-foot touches without magnetic possession.
- Argentine terminology, condensed typography and celeste/navy presentation replace generic English labels.
- The match shell keeps score and pitch visible together, with a collapsible navigation rail.
- League, async and scouting matches start at x1 by default.
