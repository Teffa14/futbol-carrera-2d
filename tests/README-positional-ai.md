# Positional AI regression scenarios

These tests cover the photographed wall-ball failure mode:

- the active ball actor can reach physical contact with a ball resting on the touchline;
- opposing actors use different approach sides rather than mirrored coordinates;
- off-ball CB, fullback, midfielder and striker targets remain role-specific;
- non-actors do not collapse onto a loose wall ball;
- the normal wall safety guard remains active for players who are not the ball actor.
