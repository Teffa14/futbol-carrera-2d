A football career simulator about earning your place

Career Eleven is a free browser football career simulator built around one player, not a manager. You join a squad that already has a coach, hierarchy and tactical model, then train and compete for minutes across a persistent career.

BROWSER BETA

Create one footballer. Train. Earn minutes. Watch autonomous 2D matches. Build a full career.

You are the player, not the manager

The coach chooses the XI. Your job is to improve, understand the role and perform well enough to keep your place. Transfers, loans, injuries, selection call-ups and contracts sit around that core fantasy.

Autonomous 2D matches

Matches are simulated with 22 agents and a free physical ball. Your preparation influences what your player perceives, chooses and executes, but you do not steer every action manually.

A long career instead of a three-minute summary

Career Eleven is designed for a persistent save that develops week by week. Training, tactical familiarity, relationships and player identity build over time.

FAQ

Is Career Eleven free?

Yes. The public beta runs in the browser.

Is Career Eleven a manager game?

No. The core campaign is the career of one footballer.
